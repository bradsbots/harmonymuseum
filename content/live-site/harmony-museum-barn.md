# Barn

## The Harmony Museum Barn

Our beautifully rustic Harmonist barn measures dates to 1805. The structure measures 42 X 64 feet (excluding the 3-foot exhibit perimeter and smallest bay). The Harmony Museum Barn accommodates about 200 people seated at tables among four bays. There is plenty of room for ceremonies and celebrations from weddings to baby showers! Space can be provided for dancing with less seating. The building offers excellent lighting, and an outside rest room facility.

Enjoy your event with multiple day rentals! Our Barn rentals run from Friday to Monday. This allows you time for decorating on Friday and setup, the actual event on Saturday, and cleanup on Sunday/Monday.

Cost to rent the Harmonist Barn is a $600 security deposit and $600 rental fee. For further information [contact us](/contact-us) or call 724-452-7341.
