# Donate

## Donate to Harmony Museum

Preserving history doesn’t come cheap. Great old buildings cost money to maintain, and heat, and cool. Great events take money to organize and promote. That’s why your financial support is so important to the Harmony Museum. Your donation keeps the lights on—not only in our buildings, but also in the bright minds of all who engage the amazing history of Harmony, Pennsylvania.  Consider a gift of support today. Honor a loved one, create a recurring Memorial donation, or help out any amount you can. We thank you. Using the donation form will take you through Paypal.

If you have a Donor Advised Fund, now is a great time to share those funds! Use the DAF link below the form to start now. We would prefer you designate funds as General Funds, but you are always free to designate them however you wish. Just know that we appreciate any gift, small – large – one time – or recurring.
