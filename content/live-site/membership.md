# Membership

## Harmony Museum Membership - Join Today!

### Benefits of Joining

- Discounted event rates
- Discounted Museum Shop purchases
- Discounted rental rates on Stewart Hall, 1805 Barn and Mennonite Meetinghouse
- Monthly newsletter
- Enjoy gatherings and special programs
- Become a volunteer for events or become a docent and share our unique history with visitors
- For high schooler’s and college students: Volunteering makes a great addition to a resume or college application and is a terrific learning experience.
- Get the satisfaction of helping with events and programs and the camaraderie that comes with lending a hand.

**Join today and become a part of something great!**
