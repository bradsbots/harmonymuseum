# Silvester

## Silvester - New Years Eve

Family fun on New Year’s Eve:

**Harmony Museum**

- Harmony Museum’s fabulous Pork & Sauerkraut dinner – German Buffet – dine in, Stewart Hall, Harmony Museum. Starts at 3:30, ends at 6.
- Dinner menu is : Roast Pork, mashed potatoes, gravy, sauerkraut, applesauce, cucumber salad, bread, ginger cookie, coffee and cider. Cost is $18 per person, payable at the door.
- Gluhwein Hut will be open, selling new mugs and refilling old ones – starting at 3:00 PM.
- Christmas Tree Toss in Harmony Museum lot. Starts at 3:00. Tree toss includes men’s division, women’s division and a kids wreath toss. Prizes given for best throws.
- Museum is open 3:00 to 5:30.

**Fun Around Town**

- 5K Run/Walk 3:30 PM & 1 Mile Fun Run 4:15 PM signup at [SIGNUP LINK](https://runsignup.com/Race/PA/Harmony/HarmonySilvester5K)
more info at www.harmonyboro-pa.us
- Music in the town square
- Sales and surprises at the specialty shops throughout town
- Ending with a ball drop and Fireworks at 6 pm midnight German time
