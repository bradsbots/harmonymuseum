All entrées include the buffet of Strawberry Salad, Roasted Red Potatoes, Vegetable Medley and fresh rolls and butter, all created by Harmony’s own Chopped Catering.
Also included is a cookie table dessert.

In the dead of winter, 1805, Father George Rapp gathered his followers together and signed papers founding the communal Harmonie Society.  Historic Harmony and the Harmony Museum celebrate that signing each year with Harmoniefest, an entertaining evening with food, awards, and a bit of history.

**The evening also includes a talk by our featured speaker, Andrea Durnell from Baden, PA. She will be speaking on the history of feed sack clothing from the 1940’s to the 1960’s, and she will present examples of feed sack clothing and their uses. **

And we’ll award this year’s Heritage Awards and Commendations, given for outstanding efforts in historic preservation.
