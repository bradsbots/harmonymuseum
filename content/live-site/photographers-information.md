# Photographers Information

## PROFESSIONAL PHOTOGRAPHER INFORMATION

We LOVE that you love Harmony Museum enough to use our properties with your clients photographs. The most popular are our barn and Mennonite meeting house, along with our gardens behind the Harmony Museum Shop.

However, there are some rules you must follow to avoid issues for us and for you.

First, some of our properties are available for photography by appointment only.
We also require insurance paperwork from you, and a small fee.

Please [download the Photographers Form](https://ayg.gen.mybluehost.me/website_94c3bb04/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/HH_Photographer_Permision_Form_Revised.pdf) and contact us to set up your dates.
