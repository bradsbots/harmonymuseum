# Vintage Harmony: A Wine & History Experience

## Vintage Harmony

Step into an evening where fine wine, rich history, and German cuisine come together in the heart of historic Harmony.

Join the Harmony Museum for Vintage Harmony: A Wine and History Experience, an exclusive event designed for those who appreciate exceptional flavors, fascinating stories, and unforgettable surroundings.

[Register Here](https://givebutter.com/vintageharmony)

Guests will begin the evening at the Woodville House showroom, where they’ll enjoy wine selections curated by wine educator and owner of Tastegreatwines, Jill M. Kummer, CPM, CHS, WSET-2, alongside exquisite German hors doeuvres prepared by Nicholas Saxon of SaxOn Food Ink. During the reception, a local historian will present the captivating story of the Harmony Short Line and its lasting impact on the town’s heritage.

The experience continues with an intimate journey to the Harmony Museum. Guests will explore the museum grounds and descend into the museum’s undercroft wine cellar — a rare opportunity to experience one of Harmony’s hidden historic treasures – where an additional presentation will be given. Food, wine, and conversation will continue in Stewart Hall, creating the perfect atmosphere to relax, socialize, and savor the evening.

With attendance limited to just 50 guests, this exclusive event is expected to sell out quickly. [Click Here](https://givebutter.com/vintageharmony) to register.
