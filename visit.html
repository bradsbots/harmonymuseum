30 minutes and 200 years from Pittsburgh

Harmony, Pennsylvania

Founded in 1804 and still alive with the big history of a small town. One thing to know before you come: the museum buildings are open only on the guided tour, at 1:00 and 2:30. The town, the walking tour and the grounds are open whenever you are.

Hours

Tuesday – Saturday, 1–4 p.m.

Guided tours only, at 1:00 and 2:30.

Closed Sunday and Monday, and December 24–26.

No reservation needed — just come for the 1:00 or the 2:30. Groups and school visits: call ahead.

Admission

Getting here

218 Mercer Street, Harmony, PA 16037

Harmony is at I-79 exits 87–88, about 10 miles north of the Pennsylvania Turnpike, 30 miles north of Pittsburgh's Point and 30 miles south of I-80. From exit 88 turn right, then left onto Mercer Road (look for the museum sign). Mercer Road becomes Mercer Street after the bridge over the Connoquenessing Creek.

Free visitor parking in the lot along the side of the building.

While you're here

The museum shop

In a separate building across the parking lot at 222 Mercer Street, so you can shop even when the museum is closed. Books, prints, and unique, affordable gifts. Members get a discount.

Tuesday – Saturday, 1–4 p.m. · members get a discount

The Weavers Cabin

At Mercer and Spring Streets, almost directly across from the Harmony Inn. Drop in, hear the history of weaving and spinning, watch the looms at work, and buy something handmade to take home. Hours are posted on the weavers' Facebook page.

The walking tour

Most of the historic buildings in town are privately owned, but you can still walk past every one of them reading what happened there. Twenty minutes for the town center, two hours for the full loop.

Open the map →

Genealogy library

Learning about your ancestors is exciting and slow. To use the library, make an appointment with the museum administrator first — if you drop in, there may be no one available. Call (724) 452-7341 or send a message.

Group and school tours

Groups are welcome by appointment outside the regular tour times. Call the office to arrange a date.

The rest of the town

While you're walking, stop in at the shops and eateries — this is small-business shopping at its best. Pick up the Harmony Business Association's brochure in most shops in town. Staying longer? The Butler County Tourism and Convention Bureau has everything else.

Harmony Museum

Historic Harmony, Inc. — a volunteer-run 501(c)(3) organized in 1943, preserving nine properties of the Harmony Society and the Mennonites who followed.
