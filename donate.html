Historic Harmony, Inc. · gifts are tax-deductible

Preserving history doesn't come cheap

Great old buildings cost money to maintain, to heat and to cool. Great events take money to organize and promote. Your gift keeps the lights on — not only in nine historic buildings, but in the minds of everyone who comes to find out what happened here.

A one-time gift

Any amount, general funds. This is what pays for the next set of windows.

In honor or in memory

Honor a loved one, or set up a recurring memorial gift. Tell us who, and we'll acknowledge it.

From a donor-advised fund

Grants from a DAF are welcome. We'd prefer they be designated as general funds, but you are always free to direct them as you wish.

Historic Harmony, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) organization. Its work is made possible by members and volunteers, and funded through fundraisers, membership and tour fees, gift-shop sales, donations, and a grant from the Pennsylvania Historical and Museum Commission, a state agency funded by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

The official registration and financial information of Historic Harmony, Inc. may be obtained from the Pennsylvania Department of State by calling toll-free, within Pennsylvania, (800) 732-0099. Registration does not imply endorsement. The organization's Form 990 can be obtained from the office at 218 Mercer Street.

Prefer to give your time? See volunteering. Prefer to give every year? Membership starts at $25.
