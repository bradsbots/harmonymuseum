Who we are

A volunteer-run museum, organized in 1943

We are Historic Harmony, Inc., a 501(c)(3) organization. We own and preserve nine properties — seven historic buildings, the Harmony Society Cemetery and the Mennonite Meetinghouse Cemetery — and through projects, activities and concerts we showcase the German culture of the settlers who came here more than 200 years ago. Our heritage crafts and music events draw some 25,000 visitors a year.

Our mission

  1. To preserve and promote knowledge of the Harmony area's history and heritage through museum collections and outreach activities.
  2. To foster tourism in southwestern Butler County.
  3. To encourage preservation of historical resources in support of educational quality of life, economic development and associated community objectives.
  4. To support students of history and museum management through paid internships.
  5. To encourage other property owners to preserve and beautify their historic buildings through our Heritage Awards Program.

What we do

Restoration

Seven of our buildings are more than 200 years old, and restoring them never ends. The most recent project replaced more than 40 windows in the 1812 Wagner/Bentel Haus, at a cost of $150,000.

Preservation

We accept donations of artifacts important to the history of Harmony and the surrounding region — many are on display in the museum — and we collect the memories and stories that bring local history to life.

Education

Through events, classes, guided tours, the walking tour, the Weavers Cabin and our archives, we share the town's history with anyone who wants it.

How we're funded

Members and volunteers make the museum possible. Money comes from fundraisers, membership and tour fees, gift-shop sales, donations, and a grant from the Pennsylvania Historical and Museum Commission, a state agency funded by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
