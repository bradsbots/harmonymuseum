What members get

  • Free admission to the museum
  • Discounted event rates
  • Discounted museum shop purchases
  • Discounted rental rates on Stewart Hall, the 1805 Barn and the Mennonite Meetinghouse
  • The members' newsletter, by mail
  • Gatherings and special programs
  • First call to volunteer for events, or to train as a docent and share the town's history with visitors
  • For high-school and college students: volunteering here is a real addition to a résumé or college application
